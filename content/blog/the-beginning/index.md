this is my first post here. this is also my first public post on the internet in a long time. in forever, really, if we discount my now decrepit facebook account that i may have posted on when i was 12 or 13. and i'll gladly discount those posts. so this is a big moment, then.

i'm not sure why this site has been brought into existence. 
maybe i think that by having someplace to put anything i write, i'll write things to be put there.

maybe, i do have some childlike fascination for the old internet of the days of yore, that magical world that i see only in video essays about how it's all gone downhill now. 
maybe, i just want to expose some of me to the world, so that talking to me is not the only way to know what i'm upto. 

maybe, i want to leave something behind - not of me as a person for whenever i inevitably die, but of the me of today. we all go through a great many small deaths before reaching that final one. there are parts of who i used to be that neither i nor anyone else remembers. 
maybe it would be nice if the i of today were to exist forevermore, enshrined in text here.

or maybe i'm just awfully bored today. and this will be the ending as well.

regardless, here i am. here we are. and here you'll find anything else i decide to share.
